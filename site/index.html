FAQ

What documents do I need to file my income tax return?

Usually your PAN and Aadhaar, bank statements, and any past returns or notices. Salaried clients also bring Form 16 and investment proofs. We will tell you exactly what applies to you.

Who needs GST registration?

Registration is required once turnover crosses the prescribed threshold, and in some cases regardless of turnover. Tell us about your business and we will confirm whether it applies to you.

I received an income tax or GST notice. What should I do?

Do not ignore it. Send us a copy. We will read it, explain it in plain language, and prepare a reply within the due date.

Do you file TDS returns?

Yes. We prepare and file TDS returns and the related certificates.
